# QA checklist: Green Room launch, 13 Nov 2026

The client's ask in docs/feedback-2026-11-13.md is the source of truth. Run every round at 390, 768 and 1440 wide, in light and dark. Each check is recorded as ✅ met, ⚠️ partly met or ❌ missing, with a note.

## A. Flow and steps (client's ask)

1. Coming soon → the main button goes straight to /join/, and its label says where it goes. There is no two-step form.
2. For professionals → every call-to-action goes to /join/, with the practical reasons right beside the button.
3. Invite email → one "Create your profile" button → /join/?invite=… with the email prefilled.
4. Sign up → the account is created → it lands in the profile editor with no dead ends.
5. Editor → what's required to submit is obvious, there is no "0%" homework bar, and optional sections are clearly marked.
6. Editor → Preview → Submit for review is one clear, visible path.
7. Submit → the "Profile submitted" page (IN REVIEW, private during review, View / Edit).
8. Confirmation email → private during review, when to expect to hear, signed "YOUR BETWEEN GIGS TEAM".
9. My profile after submitting → the status card and the full preview (CI flow).
10. Log in (returning user) → the profile/editor. The owner experience is unchanged.

## C. UI standards (senior review)

- **Alignment and layout:** alignment to a shared grid, and consistent gutters (16px on phones).
- **Type:** the serif only for display and headings, a consistent scale, no orphans in headlines, and line length of about 45–75 characters.
- **Hierarchy:** exactly one primary button per view, and the secondary actions visibly secondary.
- **Spacing:** consistent vertical spacing between sections, and no cramped or empty gaps.
- **Backgrounds:** the photo background lines up behind the content at every width. Text sits on solid or near-solid areas, and no key objects are cut off awkwardly.
- **Touch:** touch targets ≥ 44px, and no horizontal scroll.
- **Contrast and focus:** contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text, with visible focus states.
- **Forms:** labels on fields, and errors next to the field and summarised at submit.
- **States:** empty, loading, error, saved, changes requested and approved are all designed.
- **Dark mode:** every page reads well in dark.
