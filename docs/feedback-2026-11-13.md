## Gigster profile editor (Flow p3, Peter, Patti)

- **"Your name"**, not "Your public name" (Patti).
- **"How I can help" is the most important part** (Peter). Put a worked example right next to the field, e.g. Maya's: "Closet organization · I'll sort your wardrobe and set up a closet you can keep organized." One clearly described service is enough to start.
- **Services: up to 5** per Gigster (Patti).
- **Standard service descriptions** (Patti). When a Gigster picks a service category, the system shows a short standard description of that category, so Gigsters can be compared. The Gigster adds their own one-line "what you'll do" in customer words. *Open question for Justin/Patti: can a Gigster edit the standard text? Default: no.*
- Replace "What did you do in entertainment?" with **"What kind of work do you do in film and television?"**, then ask **"How does that experience help you deliver this service?"**.
- **Personality question:** instead of "What's always in your kit?", the Gigster chooses 1 of these 4 questions and answers it (Justin):
  - What's one thing on your bucket list?
  - What's a skill you don't brag about?
  - What's something people would never guess about you?
  - What's the most unexpected thing you've been asked to do on a production?
- **Make required vs optional obvious.** Don't open with "0%"; it feels like homework. Show what's needed to submit, what can be added later, and a clear path from **Preview** to **Submit for review**.
- **Clarify rates** (Peter): say what the rate applies to, how to quote a project separately (hourly vs a flat fee per service), and how the 10% platform fee affects what they take home. Show "You'd receive $X after the 10% Between Gigs fee".
- Keep everything that works today: photo + cropping, credits (pin/reorder, only pinned show), availability, earliest start, minimum, private booking contact (hidden from the public profile), IMDb optional, validation only at submit, saving as you go.
- **Data:** store new answers without breaking the live Company OS backend. Prefer existing extensible fields (e.g. profile extras) over schema changes. Note any backend field this needs.
