## Decisions (13 Nov 2026)

The four open questions, decided by the design team on the client's brief. Each is one setting, so it can change later.

1. **Mailing address:** the invented 11766 Wilshire Blvd is gone from every email; the footer reads "Between Gigs · Los Angeles, California" (`emails/config.ts` MAILING_ADDRESS). An email shouldn't print an address that isn't the company's. Before invites go out in bulk, CAN-SPAM wants a full postal address there (street, P.O. box or registered mailbox).
2. **Standard service descriptions:** not editable. They're what makes Gigsters comparable (Patti's reason for them); each Gigster's own one line beside it is theirs (`lib/profile/service-descriptions.ts`).
3. **Review time:** "within 3 business days", in the confirmation email and on /profile/submitted/ (`REVIEW_TIME`).
4. **Vetting:** the vetting today is the team's review, which the email already describes ("Our team will review your profile…"). No background check is mentioned because none exists; `VETTING_NOTE` stays empty until one does.

The profile editor's newer answers (title, one line per service, personality question and answer) now map to Company OS tenant fields, and the live emails are re-rendered for Company OS: docs/handoff/companyos-profile-extras.md.

## Gold standard (Justin, 13 Nov 2026)

Justin's Flow p3 on-page editor (Figma "Between Gigs Flow", node 65:1089) is the style every page in the editorial (Green Room) look follows:

- **Edit on the page itself.** The editor is the profile page with its fields in place (`components/gigster/editor/canvas.tsx`), not a form.
- **Rules, not cards.** Sections are separated by thin rules (`border-t border-line-strong`), with columns divided by a vertical rule. Tinted, rounded panels are kept for the single highlight on a screen (for example "You're live").
- **Serif heads.** Section titles are set in the display serif. Labels and body text are in the sans.
- **"+ Add" links** for optional parts, and dashed boxes for empty media (photo, work samples).
- **Line icons only.** No emoji or 3D art ("Might not be necessary?"). In the editorial look, `Icon3D` renders its Lucide line twin, so the other looks keep their art.
