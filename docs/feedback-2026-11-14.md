## Nav: Sign in and Sign up

- Justin asked whether visitors need separate Sign in and Sign up buttons. Yes: the common pattern for sites that
  want sign-ups is a quiet "Sign in" link plus one filled "Sign up" button, so new visitors don't hunt for a small
  link. Sign up hides on the sign-up page itself and disappears once someone is signed in.
- On phones (client: the stacked title "looks ugly") the Los Angeles Call Sheet is a slim forest band under the
  nav bar (Justin: "it should be under the nav bar"), ruled in gold like the Coming soon seal; the bar holds the
  logo, Day/Night, Sign in and Sign up (checked down to 280px). From sm the title sits in the middle of the bar as before.
